Questions

Questions cleaning businesses ask.

Can I roster cleaners across lots of client sites?

Yes. Each client site is its own location with its own location check, so your crew knows exactly where tonight's job is.

Can I keep track of Police Checks and Working with Children Checks?

Yes. Record who holds what and when it expires, and TEAM MATE warns you before you roster someone who doesn't have one.

What if a cleaner calls in sick?

Post the shift as an open shift. Free cleaners ask for it from their phone, and you pick who takes it.

Can each site have its own checklist?

Yes. Set the jobs each client expects, and your crew ticks them off on the night.
