For your business

Built around an after-hours week.

Pick "cleaning" in the welcome wizard and your crew is set up in minutes: no implementation call, no sales demo.

  • Your crew, ready to go — cleaners and team leaders, plus a typical week to start from: weeknights 6 till 10 and Saturday mornings.
  • Police Checks and Working with Children Checks — track who holds what and when it expires, and get a warning before you roster someone who doesn't have one.
  • Suggested rules for cleaning crews — a meal break once a shift runs past 4½ hours, 8 hours off between a late job and an early start, and no more than 5 days in a row. Common starting points: you check them, switch them on, and change any number.
  • Clock in at the client's site — each site gets its own location check, plus an optional photo on each clock-in, so hours match who was actually there.
  • Someone calls in sick? — post it as an open shift, free cleaners ask for it from their phone, and you pick who takes it.
  • A checklist for every site — the jobs each client expects, ticked off on the night.
  • A daily wages budget — the roster's cost bar and the live wages figure tell you when a night is running heavy.
