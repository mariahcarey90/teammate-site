For your site

Built around a site week.

Pick "construction or trades" in the welcome wizard and your crew is set up in minutes: no implementation call, no sales demo.

  • Your crew, ready to go — labourers, carpenters, plant operators and site supervisors, plus a typical week to start from: weekdays 7 till 3:30 and Saturday mornings.
  • White Cards and high-risk licences — track who holds what and when it expires, and get a warning before you roster someone who doesn't have one.
  • A suggested meal-break rule — a common starting point for days longer than 5 hours. You check it, switch it on, and change any number.
  • Clock in on site — optional location check and photo on each clock-in, so hours match who was actually on the job.
  • More than one site? — share your crew between sites when a job needs extra hands.
  • A daily wages budget — the roster's cost bar and the live wages figure tell you when a day is running heavy.
  • Announcements people confirm — new site rules, a change of start time: see who's read it.
