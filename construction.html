Questions

Questions builders and trades ask.

Can I keep track of White Cards and high-risk licences?

Yes. Record who holds what and when it expires, and TEAM MATE warns you before you roster someone who doesn't have one.

Can my crew clock in on site?

Yes. Turn on a location check and an optional photo for each clock-in, so hours match who was actually on the job.

Can I move crew between sites?

Yes. Each site is its own location, and you can share your crew between sites when a job needs extra hands.
