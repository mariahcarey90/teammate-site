Questions

Questions field service businesses ask.

Can I keep track of trade licences?

Yes. Record each electrical, plumbing, refrigerant handling (ARCtick) and pest management licence, driver licence and First Aid certificate and when it runs out, and TEAM MATE warns you before you roster someone who doesn't have what their job needs.

Can I roster an evening call-out shift?

Yes. The typical week includes one, and it's a shift like any other: on the roster, on their phone and on the timesheet.

Can it warn me when someone's back too soon after a late job?

Yes. Tick the suggested rules and TEAM MATE warns you when someone gets less than 10 hours off between shifts. You choose the number.

How do technicians see their shifts?

On their phone. Shifts land the moment you publish, with the times, and they clock in and out from the same app.
