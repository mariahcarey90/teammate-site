Questions

Questions venues ask.

Can I keep track of RSA and food-safety certificates?

Yes. Record who holds what and when it expires, and TEAM MATE warns you before you roster someone who doesn't have one.

What happens when someone calls in sick?

Offer the shift as an open shift, approve a swap, or borrow a hand from your other venue.

Can I set Saturday, Sunday and public-holiday rates?

Yes. Set your Saturday, Sunday, evening and public-holiday rates once and pay adds them up. You choose the rates.

Is it really free for a small café?

Yes, for teams of 10 or fewer, with every feature. Past 10 staff it's $60 a month for up to 30 staff.
