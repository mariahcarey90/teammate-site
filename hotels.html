Questions

Questions hotels ask.

Can it tell me when the front desk is empty?

Yes. Tick the suggested rules and the roster lists any hour with no Front Desk Agent on between 7am and 11pm, or no Night Auditor overnight. You choose the numbers and can change them.

Can I keep track of RSA and First Aid?

Yes. Record who holds what and when it expires, along with food safety, and TEAM MATE warns you before you roster someone who doesn't have one.

Do overnight shifts work?

Yes. An 11pm-to-7am night audit is one shift on the roster and one line on the timesheet.

Can housekeeping, the restaurant and the bar share one roster?

Yes. Front desk, housekeeping, restaurant, bar and kitchen are areas of one hotel, so you see the whole day at once.
