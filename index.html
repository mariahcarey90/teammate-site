Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is TEAM MATE really free?

Yes, for teams of 10 or fewer, with the whole app: rosters, time clock, timesheets, leave and messaging. It's not a trial, so there's no card and no time limit. Past 10 staff it's $60 a month for up to 30 staff, then $3 a month for each person after that.

Who is TEAM MATE for?

Australian businesses that roster shift workers. There's a ready-made setup for cafés, restaurants and bars, shops, cleaning businesses, security teams, clinics and building crews: pick yours in the welcome wizard and it adds the jobs, a typical week and suggested rules to start from.

Do I pay for managers?

No. A seat is a person on the roster, not a login, so owners and managers who don't work shifts cost nothing.

Can I run more than one location?

Yes. Each venue, store or site is its own location, and you can share staff between them when one is short.

Can I set my own weekend and public-holiday rates?

Yes. Set your weekend, evening and public-holiday rates once and pay adds them up on every shift. You choose the rates.

Does it work with my payroll software?

TEAM MATE exports approved timesheets as files your payroll software can read, including Xero-ready files.

Where is my data kept?

In Australia. TEAM MATE is made in Melbourne and hosted in Australia.
