FAQ

The short answers.

Who counts as a person?

Everyone you add to TEAM MATE: the owner, managers, admins, supervisors, staff, subcontractors, volunteers and your outside bookkeeper, whether or not they work shifts. Each person counts once, from the day you add them until you switch them off.

What happens after the free trial?

Your 14 days include every Plus feature, and you don't need a card to start. Nothing is charged automatically. We email you 7 days, 3 days and 1 day before the trial ends. To carry on, choose Foundation or Plus and add a card. If you don't, your workspace is paused: you can still see and download everything for 90 days, then it's deleted.

Can we switch plans?

Yes, from Foundation to Plus or back, in the app. If you move to Foundation, the Plus tools switch off, and your onboarding and HR records stay there to read.

Someone left. Do we still pay for them?

Switch them off in TEAM MATE and they stop counting straight away. Your settings page always shows exactly how many people you're paying for.

What does tip sharing cost?

$2.50 a person a month, on either plan. It shares each day's tips among the people who worked that day. Your business still pays the tips itself; TEAM MATE never holds the money.

Can we cancel?

Any time, online: Settings, then Manage billing, on the TEAM MATE website. There's no contract. Your plan carries on to the end of the month you've paid for, and your data stays exportable.

Is GST included?

No. Prices are in Australian dollars, and GST is added on top.

Does it handle our payroll?

TEAM MATE produces your timesheets and exports them as files your payroll software can read, including Xero-ready files. You keep your payroll system; we save you the data entry.
