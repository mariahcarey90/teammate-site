For your store

Built around a retail week.

Pick "shop or retail store" in the welcome wizard and your store is set up in minutes: no implementation call, no sales demo.

  • Shop floor, checkout and stockroom, ready to go — sales assistants, cashiers, stock hands and supervisors, plus a typical week to start your roster from.
  • Labour as a % of sales — set a target and see which days ran over.
  • Suggested rules for retail — common starting points for meal breaks, rest between shifts and days in a row. You check them, switch them on, and change any number.
  • A daily wages budget — the roster's cost bar and the live wages figure tell you when a day is running heavy.
  • Opening and closing checklists — recurring tasks your crew ticks off in the app, every shift.
  • More than one store? — share staff between your stores when a shift needs cover.
  • Announcements people confirm — new promo, new process: see who's read it.
