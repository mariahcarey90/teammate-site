Questions

Questions shops and stores ask.

Can I roster to my sales?

Yes. Upload daily sales and TEAM MATE suggests a headcount for mornings, peak and evenings. Set a labour target as a % of sales and see which days ran over.

How do casuals pick up extra shifts?

Staff set their availability, and extra shifts go out as open shifts they can claim in a tap.

Can I share staff between stores?

Yes. Each store is its own location, and you can share staff between them when a shift needs cover.
