For your business

Built around a 24/7 week.

Pick "security" in the welcome wizard and your team is set up in minutes: no implementation call, no sales demo.

  • Your team, ready to go — security officers, crowd controllers, control room operators and supervisors, plus a typical week to start from: three 8-hour shifts a day, every day, 6 till 2, 2 till 10 and 10 till 6.
  • Security and crowd controller licences — track who holds what and when it expires, along with First Aid and Police Checks, and get a warning before you roster someone who doesn't have one.
  • Suggested rules for security — a meal break on shifts longer than 5 hours, 8 hours off between shifts, and no shift longer than 10½ hours. Common starting points: you check them, switch them on, and change any number.
  • Night shifts that cross midnight — a 10pm-to-6am shift is one shift on the roster and one line on the timesheet.
  • Clock in at the post — each site gets its own location check, plus an optional photo on each clock-in, so hours match who was actually there.
  • Handover notes — what the night shift wants the morning shift to know.
  • Someone calls in sick? — post it as an open shift, free officers ask for it from their phone, and you pick who takes it.
  • A daily wages budget — the roster's cost bar and the live wages figure tell you when a day is running heavy.
