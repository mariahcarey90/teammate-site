Questions

Questions security teams ask.

Can I keep track of security and crowd controller licences?

Yes. Record who holds what and when it expires, along with First Aid and Police Checks, and TEAM MATE warns you before you roster someone who doesn't have one.

Do overnight shifts work?

Yes. A 10pm-to-6am shift is one shift on the roster and one line on the timesheet.

How do I know officers clocked in at the right post?

Each site gets its own location check, plus an optional photo on each clock-in, so hours match who was actually there.

Can the night shift leave notes for the morning?

Yes. Handover notes carry what the night shift wants the morning shift to know.
