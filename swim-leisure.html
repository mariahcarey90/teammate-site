For your centre

Built around a pool that’s open seven days.

Pick "Swim or leisure centre" in the welcome wizard and your team is set up in minutes: no implementation call, no sales demo.

  • Your team, ready to go Lifeguards, swim teachers, receptionists, a fitness instructor, café staff and duty managers, plus a typical week to start from: open 5:30am to 8:30pm on weekdays and 7am to 6pm at weekends, two lifeguards on the pool deck the whole time with overlapping handovers, and swim lessons after school and on Saturday morning.
  • Two lifeguards on the pool deck Tick the suggested rules and TEAM MATE warns you when fewer than 2 people with a Pool Lifeguard ticket are rostered on the pool deck while you’re open. You choose the numbers; TEAM MATE doesn’t check them against any law or pool safety guideline.
  • Lifeguard and swim teacher tickets Track who holds what and when it runs out: Pool Lifeguard, swim teacher qualifications, First Aid, CPR, Working with Children Checks, Certificate III in Fitness and Pool Operations Certificates. You get a warning before you roster someone without one.
  • Suggested rules for swim centres A meal break on shifts longer than 5 hours, 10 hours off between shifts and no shift longer than 10½ hours. No shortest shift, so short lesson blocks don’t set off warnings. Common starting points: you check them, switch them on, and change any number.
  • Swim lessons on the roster An after-school block of lessons or a Saturday morning is a shift like any other, in the Learn to Swim area of your roster.
  • Handover notes What the morning lifeguards want the afternoon shift to know.
  • Someone calls in sick? Post it as an open shift, free lifeguards ask for it from their phone, and you pick who takes it.
  • Leave without the paperwork Staff request leave from their phone and see their balances, and approved leave shows on the roster before you schedule over it.

Questions

Questions swim centres ask.

Can it tell me when the pool deck is short?

Yes. Tick the suggested rules and the roster lists any time fewer than 2 people with a Pool Lifeguard ticket are on the pool deck while you're open. You choose the numbers; TEAM MATE doesn't check them against any law or pool safety guideline.

Can I keep track of lifeguard and swim teacher tickets?

Yes. Record each Pool Lifeguard ticket, swim teacher qualification, First Aid, CPR, Working with Children Check and Pool Operations Certificate, and when each one runs out, and TEAM MATE warns you before you roster someone who doesn't have what their job needs.

Do short lesson shifts work?

Yes. An after-school block of swim lessons or a Saturday morning is a shift like any other: on the roster, on their phone and on the timesheet.

How do lifeguards see their shifts?

On their phone. Shifts land the moment you publish, with the area and the times, and they clock in and out from the same app.
