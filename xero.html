TEAM MATE and Xero Payroll

TEAM MATE sends each person's approved hours to Xero Payroll as a draft timesheet, so nobody types hours in twice. You still check and approve every timesheet in Xero, and run your pay run there as usual.

What goes where

Most information flows one way, from TEAM MATE into Xero. TEAM MATE reads a few things from Xero so it can put your hours in the right place.

TEAM MATE never changes or deletes an existing Xero employee, never runs a pay run, and never moves money.

Getting started

You need a TEAM MATE admin login and a Xero login that can manage payroll for your organisation. Payroll must be switched on in Xero, with at least one pay calendar.

  1. In TEAM MATE on the web, go to Settings → Integrations.
  2. Under Xero Payroll, press Connect to Xero.
  3. Xero opens in the same window. Sign in, pick your organisation, and press Allow access. Xero lists exactly what TEAM MATE may do: read and add employees, read and add pay items, read pay calendars, and create draft timesheets.
  4. You're sent back to TEAM MATE, which shows your organisation's name and "Connected".
  5. Pick the Xero pay items for ordinary hours and overtime. TEAM MATE never guesses these. Nothing is sent until ordinary hours has a pay item, and anyone with overtime waits until overtime has one too.

New to TEAM MATE and already use Xero? On our sign-up page, choose "Fill this in from your Xero login" and we'll fill in your name and email from Xero. You connect your organisation afterwards, as above.

Setting up pay items

Xero works out pay from its own pay items. Each penalty rate you've switched on in TEAM MATE (for example Saturday, Sunday, public holiday or evening) needs a matching Xero pay item, and the rate on that pay item must match the rate you set in TEAM MATE.

  • Pick one you already have: Settings → Integrations shows a picker for each penalty rate in use.
  • Or let TEAM MATE add them: press "Create missing pay items". You see exactly what will be added (for example "Saturday 1.25x (TEAM MATE)") before anything happens, and nothing is added until you press "Create in Xero".

Until a penalty rate has a pay item, anyone with those hours shows "Needs penalty pay item" and isn't sent, so nobody is underpaid by having penalty hours paid as ordinary hours.

Tips: if a location shares tips through TEAM MATE, you can also send each person's share as a Tips line. Set up a pay item in Xero at $1.00 per unit, and TEAM MATE sends one unit per dollar.

Matching and adding staff

TEAM MATE matches your staff to Xero employees by email address, or by exact name. Anyone it can't match shows "Not in Xero". You can link them by hand under Match staff, or add them with Add new staff to Xero, which sends the details they entered when they joined (name, date of birth, home address, tax file number, bank account, super fund). Xero needs a date of birth and a home address written "street, suburb STATE postcode". Anyone without these is held back until they're fixed. You finish the rest of the tax declaration in Xero.

Sending timesheets

  1. Approve the week's timesheets in TEAM MATE as usual.
  2. On the Timesheets page, press Send to Xero. TEAM MATE uses each person's Xero pay calendar to work out the pay period.
  3. A preview lists each person, their hours split the way Xero will get them, and whether they're ready, already sent, changed since sent, or can't be sent (with the reason).
  4. Press send. Each person gets one draft timesheet per pay period in Xero.
  5. In Xero, check and approve the timesheets, then run your pay run.

Questions

When does TEAM MATE send to Xero?

Only when a manager presses Send to Xero. There's no automatic or overnight sync.

Can the same hours be sent twice?

No. TEAM MATE remembers what it sent for each person and pay period. If approved hours change after sending, the preview says "Changed since sent", and sending again updates the same Xero timesheet. If you had already approved it in Xero, it goes back to draft for you to approve again. A timesheet Xero has already paid can't be changed.

What doesn't TEAM MATE do?

It doesn't run pay runs, work out tax or super, or pay anyone. It doesn't read your accounts, invoices or bank feeds. It doesn't change or delete existing Xero employees; once someone is in Xero, update their bank details there.

Xero says payroll isn't set up

Switch on Payroll in your Xero organisation and add a pay calendar, then try again.

A send failed. What now?

The preview shows the reason Xero gave next to each person, for example a missing pay item or a person not in Xero. Fix that and send again; people already sent are skipped.

How do I disconnect from Xero?

Settings → Integrations → Disconnect from Xero. Timesheets already sent stay in Xero. You can also remove TEAM MATE in Xero under Settings → Connected apps; TEAM MATE then shows "Needs reconnecting". If you close your TEAM MATE account, TEAM MATE disconnects from Xero for you.

Can I connect more than one Xero organisation?

One TEAM MATE business connects to one Xero organisation.
